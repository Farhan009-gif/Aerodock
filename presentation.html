Key Features

Runway is designed to simplify your daily life and help you stay organized

Task Management

Create, organize, and prioritize your daily tasks with our intuitive interface. Add due dates, set reminders, and track your progress with visual indicators.

Goal Tracking

Set long-term goals and break them down into actionable steps. Runway helps you measure your progress and celebrate your achievements along the way.

Productivity Analytics

Gain insights into your productivity patterns with detailed analytics. Understand when you're most productive and optimize your schedule accordingly.

Smart Reminders

Never forget important tasks again. Runway sends intelligent reminders based on your schedule and habits, helping you stay on top of your responsibilities.

How Runway Works

A simple, intuitive workflow designed for everyday use

1

Set Your Goals

Begin by defining what you want to achieve. Runway helps you break down big goals into manageable tasks.

2

Create Tasks

Create specific, actionable tasks that will help you reach your goals. Assign priorities and deadlines.

3

Track Progress

As you complete tasks, watch your progress bar fill up. Celebrate small wins along the way.

4

Review & Reflect

At the end of each week, review your accomplishments and reflect on what worked and what didn't.

Why This Tech Stack?

Runway was built with modern Android development best practices. I chose Kotlin and Jetpack Compose because they make app development faster and more intuitive. Room Database provides reliable local storage, while ViewModel ensures data persists through configuration changes. I use GitHub to track my progress, collaborate with others, and showcase my work. Every commit tells a story of my growth as a developer. This app represents my journey as a young developer learning new skills every day. It's not perfect, but it's mine - built with passion, patience, and determination.
